# 9618 Paper 2 Practice

Practice site for Cambridge International AS & A Level Computer Science (9618) **Paper 2: Fundamental Problem-solving and Programming Skills**.

- **Practice**: past-paper questions grouped by chapter, one at a time
- **Mock exam**: a whole paper under timed conditions
- **Master guide**: pseudocode and topic reference
- **My answers / My weak spots**: your attempts and the topics you lose marks on
- **Grade check**: estimate an A-level grade from paper marks

The site is a single static `index.html`. Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage).
AI marking and the AI tutor are not active in this static version, so you mark your own answers against the mark scheme. A backend/database is planned.

Run locally: open `index.html` in a browser.
